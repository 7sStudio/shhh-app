Ever been at work or on the bus, opened that one video, and suddenly the whole room knows what you're watching?

You put your phone on vibrate… except media volume is still blasting. 🙃

So: <b>Shhh</b> 🤫

<b>One tap</b> — ringer to vibrate, media volume to zero.
<b>Tap again</b> — everything comes back exactly how it was.

Also has:

<ul>
<li>⏱️ <b>Timed hush</b> — 15 min / 30 min / 1 h / 2 h; sound returns on its own, with an optional live countdown in the status bar</li>
<li>🌙 <b>Quiet hours</b> — automatic schedule with start/end times and per-weekday control</li>
<li>📱 <b>Quick Settings tile</b> + home screen widget</li>
<li>🎧 <b>Headphones option</b> — media un-mutes when Bluetooth headphones connect; the ringer stays hushed</li>
<li>🤖 <b>Automation friendly</b> — Tasker, MacroDroid and routines can trigger it</li>
<li>🚀 <b>Launcher shortcuts</b> — long-press the icon: Toggle · Hush 30 min · Hush 1 hour</li>
<li>🌍 <b>Thirteen languages</b>, full RTL support</li>
</ul>

Open source, free, ad-free. No accounts, no analytics. The network is touched only if you turn on the optional update check — and then only to ask GitHub for the latest release. Shhh never changes Do Not Disturb and never touches your alarms.

Basically: "please don't let my phone embarrass me today" — as an app.
