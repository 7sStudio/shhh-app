Shhh is a tiny, modern quiet-mode toggle for the moments you need silence now: meetings, movies, sleep.

One tap — from a Quick Settings tile, a home screen widget, or the app — and your ringer switches to vibrate (or silent) while media volume drops to zero. Tap again and everything comes back, including the exact media volume you had before.

<b>Features</b>

• Quick Settings tile with live on/off state
• Home screen widget that matches your wallpaper colors (Material You)
• Timed hush: 15 min to 2 hours, sound returns on its own
• Quiet hours: automatic nightly schedule, per weekday
• Live countdown notification while a timer runs (optional)
• Bluetooth headphones option: media comes back when you connect, ringer stays hushed
• Launcher shortcuts and automation intents for Tasker & routines
• Choose vibrate or silent, and how volume is restored

<b>Private by design</b>

No accounts, no ads, no analytics — Shhh does not even have the internet permission. Open source under Apache-2.0.

<b>Access it needs (all official Android switches)</b>

• Do Not Disturb access — required by Android to switch ringer modes
• Alarms & reminders — so timers and quiet hours fire at the exact minute
• Notifications — only for the optional countdown
• Nearby devices — only for the optional Bluetooth headphones feature
