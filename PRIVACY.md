# Privacy Policy — Shhh

**Shhh collects no data. None.**

- No internet permission: the app is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no accounts.
- All settings (your schedule, volume preferences) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

The permissions Shhh requests (Do Not Disturb access, Alarms & reminders, Notifications, Nearby devices) are used exclusively on-device to switch ringer modes, fire timers, show the optional countdown, and detect Bluetooth headphones. Details: see the README.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/7sStudio/shhh-app/issues
